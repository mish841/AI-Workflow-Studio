Northstar Technologies
Internal writing standard
Document ID: NST-COM-001
Owner: Communications
Version: 1.0

1. Required metadata
Every official announcement must include Title, Audience, Owner, and Effective date in the header.

2. Required sections
The body must include these headings: Purpose, Guidance, and Contact.

3. Prohibited terminology
Do not use synergy, best-of-breed, or world-class. Prefer specific descriptions of the change.

4. Audience
Write for employees and contractors. Do not address customers directly in internal notices.
